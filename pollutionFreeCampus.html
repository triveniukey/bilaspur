Pollution-Free Campus

One of the most remarkable features about Maharishi Vidya Mandir schools is that campuses are located out of pollution in a peaceful atmosphere. Every campus of MVM schools is pollution-free. In order to support the healthy lifestyle, smoking and uses of alcohol and tobacco products are totally banned in our campus with additional support of lot of greenery.
