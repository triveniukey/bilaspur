About

Welcome to The Maharishi Vidya Mandir School - Bilaspur

Maharishi Vidya Mandir (MVM) School, Bilaspur is a part of Maharishi Global Education Movement. In India Maharishi Vidya Mandir School chain is one of the largest school systems with 150 branches in 14 states. About 6500 teaching, administrative and support staff are busy training as many as 85000 students from KG to 12th standard. The school is running under the Society MAHARISHI SHIKSHA SANSTHAN and the Registration is valid upto 99 years.

Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Bilaspur has been established in the year 1992 and is situated Maharishi Vidya Mandir - I area of Bilaspur in 2.85 acres of land with vast infrastructure, state of the art facilities including audio visual teaching system, computers, library, reading room, music room, art room, Transcendental Meditation hall, yoga hall etc.

Consciousness is the prime mover of life. Maharishi Vidya Mandir Schools are offering complete theoretical and practical knowledge of consciousness, which will open the gate of all possibilities in the life of the students. Through the technology of Maharishi Vedic Science, the schools offer mastery in the field of consciousness, enabling the student to accomplish anything and live a complete life

Message from the Principal

Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Bilaspur

Established in 1992, Maharishi Vidya Mandir, Bilaspur is one of the leading CBSE schools in Chhattisgarh. The school is managed by Maharishi Shiksha Sansthan and is part of the renowned Maharishi Global Education Movement. Spread across a spacious 2.85-acre campus, the school provides a modern learning environment with well-equipped classrooms, science laboratories, library, computer labs, sports facilities, yoga and meditation halls, and activity-based education. Our mission is to nurture academic excellence, strong character, creativity, and leadership while promoting holistic development through value-based education.
